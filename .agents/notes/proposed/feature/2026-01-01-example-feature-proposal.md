## Problem

Explain why the change matters. Write the motivation so it stands without the
solution: the reader should understand the problem before reading the proposal.

## Proposal

Describe the intended change. Plans, migration steps, and open questions belong
here while the work is unbuilt.

## Alternatives considered

**Alternative one.** State the alternative and why it lost. Record every genuine
alternative that was considered; never invent one.

## Acceptance criteria

- List the observable state that means the work is done.

## Risks

Cover both what could go wrong and what the change knowingly gives up.
